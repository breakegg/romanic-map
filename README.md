# ROMANIC-MAP

ROMANIC-MAP is an interactive map of the pharmaceutical industry, drawn in the style of a fantasy game world. Instead of kingdoms and battlefields, it charts the real landscape of drug development: who is developing what, for which disease, and how far each drug has come.

## The continent

The map is divided into seven regions, one for each major field of medicine:

- **R — Rare Diseases**
- **O — Oncology**
- **M — Metabolism**
- **A — Autoimmune & Inflammation**
- **N — Neurology**
- **I — Infectious Diseases**
- **C — Cardiovascular**

Each region is made up of territories, and each territory is a disease — obesity, diabetes, lung cancer, Alzheimer's, and many more. Territory size reflects the number of patients, so the map shows at a glance where the greatest medical needs are.

## How to explore

Click any disease territory and a full pipeline table opens below the map: every drug in development for that disease, with its company, target, modality, trial phase, approval status, patent expiry, and source links. A leaderboard ranks the world's top 50 pharmaceutical companies by prescription sales, and clicking a company shows its entire pipeline across all seven regions.

## Why a map?

The drug industry is enormous and hard to follow — thousands of drugs, hundreds of companies, and news scattered everywhere. A map turns that complexity into something you can see and explore. Whether you are a student, a researcher, or simply curious about the next breakthrough medicine, ROMANIC-MAP lets you navigate the whole landscape in one place.

## Data

Pipeline data is compiled from public sources, including FDA labels and ClinicalTrials.gov, and refreshed monthly with new approvals, Phase III results, and setbacks. Figures that could not be verified are clearly marked.
